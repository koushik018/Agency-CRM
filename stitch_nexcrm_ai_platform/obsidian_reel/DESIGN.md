## Brand & Style

This design system embodies a high-end, tactile, and cinematic workspace tailored for a boutique three-partner production house spanning ad filmmaking, commercial editing, and spatial audio engineering. The aesthetic pairs precision workstation tooling with atmospheric, gallery-grade presentation.

### Personality & Emotional Response
The interface evokes hyper-competence, luxury creative capability, and focused control. It feels less like an administrative enterprise ledger and more like a high-end color grading suite or DAW console: focused, low eye-fatigue, responsive, and tactile.

### Design Movement
**Cinematic Glass & Deep Slate Surface Tooling:**
- **Atmospheric Depth:** Layered midnight slates create infinite visual recess, allowing high-resolution footage, waveform renders, and color-coded pipelines to stand out.
- **Micro-Luminescent Borders:** Frosted, semi-translucent linear strokes delineate surfaces without heavy container fills.
- **Discipline-Specific Luminescence:** Vivid neon accents designate disciplines—Electric Violet for directing/ad filmmaking, Cyan for offline/online edit, Emerald for audio mixing/mastering, and Amber for commercial deals and delivery pipelines.

## Colors

The palette establishes an ultra-deep canvas to maximize media contrast while distributing specialized functional accents across partners and workflow states.

### Core Canvas & Neutral Palette
- **Base Canvas (`#0B0F19`):** Absolute canvas depth, grounding side rails, master background, and full-screen preview overlays.
- **Card & Container Fill (`#111827`):** Elevated baseline surface for cards, kanban wells, and active inspector docks.
- **Surface Elevation High (`#1F2937`):** Hover states, flyout menus, and active tool switches.
- **Border Surface Ghost (`rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.08)`):** Default hairline delineation.
- **Text High Contrast (`#F9FAFB`):** Primary headings, critical metrics, and project titles.
- **Text Muted (`#9CA3AF`):** Secondary metadata, timecode specs, partner tags, and sub-labels.

### Partner & Media Accents
- **Electric Violet (`#8B5CF6`):** Film Direction & Commercial Shoots. Used for primary interactive triggers, active timeline playheads, and partner 1 attribution.
- **Cyan (`#06B6D4`):** Video Editing & Color Grading. Used for partner 2 attribution, clip markers, render queues, and cut revisions.
- **Emerald (`#10B981`):** Audio Engineering & Spatial Sound. Used for partner 3 attribution, audio channel meters, approved masters, and invoice confirmations.
- **Amber (`#F59E0B`):** Pitch decks, contract negotiation stages, active alerts, and pending deliverables.

## Typography

The type system blends contemporary clarity via **Plus Jakarta Sans** with precision monospaced telemetry via **JetBrains Mono**.

### Structural Rationale
- **Editorial Proportions:** Headings use tight negative tracking (`-0.02em` to `-0.03em`) and heavy weights to match cinematic credits and brand campaign title cards.
- **Editorial Body Work:** Body copy maintains generous x-height and open letterforms for crisp legibility over dark slate backgrounds.
- **Technical Readouts:** Timecodes, frame rates (e.g., `23.98 FPS`), bitrates, asset UUIDs, and sample rates (e.g., `48kHz / 24-bit`) render strictly in **JetBrains Mono** to guarantee tabular horizontal alignment across media inspectors.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout is built around a dense, high-utility fluid workstation architecture divided into three spatial tiers: Navigation Rail (collapsible), Main Production Stage (Kanban / Grid / Table view), and Contextual Inspector (sliding drawer).

### Grid & Ergonomics
- **Desktop (>= 1280px):** Dynamic fluid grid. 12 columns with `1.25rem` gutters and outer margins fixed at `2rem`. The primary dashboard operates as an edge-to-edge canvas with split modules (deals list 4 cols, active reels 8 cols).
- **Tablet (768px - 1279px):** 8-column layout. Gutter shrinks to `1rem`; the right-side media inspector collapses into an overlay modal sheet.
- **Mobile (< 768px):** 4-column flow with `0.75rem` gutters and `1rem` safe margins. Media cards reflow into vertical aspect ratios; quick partner toggle filters switch to horizontal swipe carousels.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy does not rely on heavy drop shadows, which can muddy dark UIs. Instead, depth is articulated through translucent surface fills and subtle perimeter luminosity.

### Layering Rules
- **Base Level (`#0B0F19`):** Canvas backdrop.
- **Card Tier 1 (Surface Rest):** Background of `rgba(17, 24, 39, 0.75)` with a `1px` border of `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.08)` and `backdrop-filter: blur(12px)`.
- **Card Tier 2 (Surface Hover & Focus):** Background shifts to `rgba(31, 41, 55, 0.85)` with a hairline highlight border of `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.18)` and an ambient, partner-tinted rim glow (`box-shadow: 0 0 20px -4px rgba(139, 92, 246, 0.15)` for film, or cyan/emerald equivalents).
- **Floating Modals & Overlays:** Background of `rgba(11, 15, 25, 0.94)`, border of `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.12)`, and a deep structural drop shadow (`0 24px 48px -12px rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.7)`).

## Shapes

The system uses a balanced curvature language (level `2`): structural enough to evoke pro-audio hardware and grading monitors, yet smooth enough for modern software ergonomics.

### Curvature Tokens
- **Micro UI (`4px` / `rounded-xs`):** Status indicator dots, timecode badges, codec tags.
- **Base Components (`8px` / `0.5rem`):** Buttons, text inputs, table row highlights, partner attribution pills.
- **Cards & Surfaces (`16px` / `1rem` / `rounded-lg`):** Project tiles, video preview cards, client dossiers, analytics widgets.
- **Media Floating Panels (`24px` / `1.5rem` / `rounded-xl`):** Flyout drawers, audio visualizer docks, modal containers.

## Components

### Buttons & Interactive Controls
- **Primary Action (Brand Violet):** Solid `#8B5CF6` fill, `#FFFFFF` text, `font-weight: 600`. On hover: `#7C3AED` with a soft violet shadow (`0 0 16px rgba(139, 92, 246, 0.4)`).
- **Secondary Glass Trigger:** Semi-transparent background `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.05)`, border `1px solid rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.1)`. On hover: `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.1)`.
- **Destructive Action:** Subtle dark crimson surface with a neon coral stroke (`#EF4444`).

### Visual Media Cards
- **Header Section:** Houses the Client Name, Country Flag emoji or SVG badge, and Stage status (e.g., *Pitch*, *Rough Cut*, *Sound Mix*, *Final Master*).
- **Aspect-Ratio Media Well:** 16:9 cinematic container with thumbnail scrub capabilities, resolution badge (`4K RAW`, `ProRes 4444`, `Dolby Atmos`), and running timecode in JetBrains Mono.
- **Footer Attribution Bar:** Shows active Partner Badges with designated discipline avatars:
  - *Partner 1 (Director)*: Violet pill border.
  - *Partner 2 (Editor)*: Cyan pill border.
  - *Partner 3 (Audio Engineer)*: Emerald pill border.

### Badges & Client Tags
- **Pill Architecture:** `height: 22px`, `padding: 0 8px`, `border-radius: 9999px`.
- **Palette Discipline:** Monochromatic text with colored translucent fills:
  - Deliverable Format: `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.06)` fill, muted gray label.
  - Urgent Delivery: `rgba(245, 158, 11, 0.15)` fill, `#F59E0B` text.
  - Approved / Invoiced: `rgba(16, 185, 129, 0.15)` fill, `#10B981` text.

### Form Inputs & Search Fields
- **Container:** Dark slate input (`#0B0F19`), inset `1px` border `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.1)`.
- **Focus State:** Border transitions to `#8B5CF6` with an inner ring glow. Monospaced indicators display search shortcuts (e.g., `⌘K`).

### Selection Controls (Checkboxes & Radios)
- **Checkboxes:** Squared with `4px` radius, hairline border. Checked state fills with `#8B5CF6` and renders an SVG checkmark.
- **Radio Buttons:** Circular with dual-ring glowing center matching the active partner’s discipline accent.

### Domain-Specific Components
- **Audio Scrubber Pill:** Mini inline audio waveform that displays master stereo levels and peak hold clips.
- **Multi-Partner Pipeline Matrix:** Visual split progress bar segmenting Film (Violet), Edit (Cyan), and Mix (Emerald) completion percentages.
